# Tests

## Test 1 - Complete a sandbox bank transfer [sanity]
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: home service hub, send-money recipient and amount steps, transfer review, protected backend transfer write, sandbox result, balance update, activity update
Description: Verifies the primary money-movement journey is fast, explicit and never represents the sandbox as real settlement.
Steps:
1. Open the BH'S dashboard
2. Tap Send money
3. Enter recipient "Ada Nwosu"
4. Enter amount "25000"
5. Continue to review and confirm the sandbox transfer
Expected: A review step shows recipient, amount, currency and sandbox status; after confirmation the result is successful, the balance decreases by ₦25,000, and a new Transfer sent activity entry for Ada Nwosu appears.

## Test 2 - Use the Nigerian service hub
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: Airtime, Data, Electricity, TV, International and Multi-currency service entry points, thumb-friendly quick actions
Description: Verifies everyday Nigerian fintech services are discoverable from Home without pretending external billers are connected.
Steps:
1. Open Home
2. Tap Data
3. Tap Electricity
4. Tap TV
5. Tap International
Expected: Each service opens a clear BH'S flow notice or surface that identifies the feature and, where relevant, states it is sandbox/unavailable until a provider is connected.

## Test 3 - Manage wallets and Safe & Spend
Viewport: desktop (1280x800)
Covers: NGN/USD/GBP/EUR wallet views, add/withdraw/convert entry points, Safe & Spend separation
Description: Verifies the Wallet area presents multi-currency balances and production-shaped wallet controls without claiming live FX settlement.
Steps:
1. Select Wallet
2. Confirm NGN, USD, GBP and EUR wallets are visible
3. Open Safe & Spend
4. Select Convert
Expected: Wallet balances and Safe & Spend controls are visible, and Convert is clearly presented as a sandbox/future-provider action.

## Test 5 - Block invalid transfer and show safe failure state
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: transfer validation guardrail, insufficient-balance protection, failed-state presentation, invalid-write prevention
Description: Verifies unsafe or invalid money movement is stopped before a transfer is recorded.
Steps:
1. Open Send money
2. Leave the recipient empty and enter amount "0"
3. Submit
Expected: A visible validation message appears, the transfer sheet remains open, and no new transaction is added.
4. Enter recipient "Test Recipient" and an amount greater than the available NGN balance
5. Submit
Expected: A visible insufficient-balance error appears and no transfer is created.
